Pin-by-Pin Wiring & Net Connection Map

================================================================================
POWER & REGULATION PATH
================================================================================
[LiPo Battery 7.4V (+)]  ------> [Master Rocker Switch] ------> [LM2596 IN+]
                                                         |----> [TB6612FNG VM]

[LiPo Battery 7.4V (-)]  -------------------------------------> [COMMON GND RAIL]

[LM2596 OUT+]            -- (Calibrated to 5.0V DC) ----------> [ESP32 5V Pin]
                                                         |----> [TB6612FNG VCC]
                                                         |----> [QTR-8RC VCC]

================================================================================
ESP32 MICROCONTROLLER TO MOTOR DRIVER (TB6612FNG)
================================================================================
ESP32 GPIO 14 (PWM)       ------> TB6612FNG PWMA
ESP32 GPIO 26             ------> TB6612FNG AIN1
ESP32 GPIO 27             ------> TB6612FNG AIN2
ESP32 GPIO 25 (PWM)       ------> TB6612FNG PWMB
ESP32 GPIO 12             ------> TB6612FNG BIN1
ESP32 GPIO 13             ------> TB6612FNG BIN2
ESP32 GPIO 33             ------> TB6612FNG STBY
ESP32 GND Pin             ------> COMMON GND RAIL

TB6612FNG AO1 / AO2       ------> Left N20 Gearmotor
TB6612FNG BO1 / BO2       ------> Right N20 Gearmotor

================================================================================
ESP32 MICROCONTROLLER TO SENSOR ARRAY (POLOLU QTR-8RC)
================================================================================
QTR-8RC Sensor 1 (Far L)  ------> ESP32 GPIO 15
QTR-8RC Sensor 2          ------> ESP32 GPIO 2
QTR-8RC Sensor 3          ------> ESP32 GPIO 4
QTR-8RC Sensor 4          ------> ESP32 GPIO 16
QTR-8RC Sensor 5          ------> ESP32 GPIO 17
QTR-8RC Sensor 6          ------> ESP32 GPIO 5
QTR-8RC Sensor 7          ------> ESP32 GPIO 18
QTR-8RC Sensor 8 (Far R)  ------> ESP32 GPIO 19

QTR-8RC VCC               ------> +5.0V Regulated Rail
QTR-8RC GND               ------> COMMON GND RAIL
